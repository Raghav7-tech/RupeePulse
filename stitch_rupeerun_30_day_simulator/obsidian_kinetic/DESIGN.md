## Brand & Style

This design system targets digital-first, financially ambitious Indian Gen Z and young millennials who demand institutional capability paired with high-velocity, editorial flair. The aesthetic merges obsidian dark-mode minimalism with kinetic neon energy—taking direct cues from modern neo-banking and consumer tech powerhouses.

### Key Attributes
- **Electric Precision:** Razor-sharp typography and hyper-visible neon green accents evoke real-time kinetic execution rather than sluggish legacy banking.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Multi-tiered slate surfaces and subtle edge refractions mirror tactile obsidian slabs, framing numbers with clarity.
- **Unapologetic Confidence:** Bold contrast, intentional micro-copy, and purposeful metric callouts transform personal wealth management into a status-driven experience.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep charcoal-obsidian foundation layered with electric lime and emerald greens to create high-contrast focal points. Semantic colors handle financial states, portfolio movements, and risk thresholds.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#090D12` (Ultra-deep substrate)
  - Surface Tier 1 (Card Default): `#111720`
  - Surface Tier 2 (Card Elevated / Modal): `#182230`
  - Surface Glass: `rgba(24, 34, 48, 0.65)` with fine stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Brand & Accents:**
  - Primary Hyper-Lime: `#00F59B` (CTAs, primary focus, positive yield spikes)
  - Secondary Emerald: `#10B981` (Grounded wealth badges, sustained uptrends)
  - Tertiary Electric Indigo: `#6366F1` (Credit cards, alternate asset pools, UPI insights)
- **Semantic Risk & Status:**
  - Safe / Positive Yield: `#00F59B` / `#10B981`
  - Amber Watch / Volatility: `#F59E0B`
  - Coral Alert / Debt Spike: `#FF4B55`
  - Neutral Data Grey: `#94A3B8`
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - On Dark High-Emphasis (₹ Currency, Primary Headings): `#F8FAFC`
  - On Dark Medium-Emphasis (Subtext, Category Labels): `#94A3B8`
  - On Dark Low-Emphasis (Borders, Inactive Elements): `#475569`

## Typography

The type system pairs **Outfit** for high-impact metric values, large titles, and monetary figures with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for functional UI clarity, micro-labels, and transaction feeds.

### Usage Directives
- **Indian Rupee (₹) Formatting:** The currency symbol must match the font weight and sizing of the immediate adjacent numerical string. Never use a generic sans-serif fallback for ₹.
- **Tabular Numerics:** For real-time stock balances, UPI spend trackers, and metric rollups, enforce `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1` to prevent jitter on ticking animations.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Sub-labels, risk tags, and asset classes use `label-md` or `label-lg` with uppercase transformation and slight tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid with a responsive 12-column layout on desktop, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width `1280px`, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with dynamic bottom safe-area adjustments for bottom sheets and action bars, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Stack metric breakdowns with compact gaps (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).
- Group modular financial widgets with standard card padding (`space-lg`).
- Isolate independent functional sections (e.g., Credit Score vs Portfolio vs UPI Pay) with macro spacing (`space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface stacking combined with targeted neon-glow bloom and fine glass refraction edges rather than murky drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Surface Level 0 (Ground):** `#090D12` solid slate. No border, no elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Static Cards & Containers):** `#111720` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Subtle ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Glass Floating Modals & Sheets):** Backdrop blur of `16px`, fill `rgba(24, 34, 48, 0.70)`, and top edge stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Bottom edge stroke drops to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` to emulate directional overhead lighting.
- **Accent Glow Tiers:** Interactive highlights and active state cards emit a localized, low-opacity radial bloom:
  - Lime Focus: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.22)`
  - Coral Risk Alert: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(255, 75, 85, 0.25)`

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp technical precision with modern, touchable ergonomics. 

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Widgets & Portfolio Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) produces a sleek, refined card profile that prevents clunky edges.
- **Sheets & Elevated Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) for expansive bottom sheets and full-context investment overviews.
- **Interactive Controls & Mini Badges (`rounded`):** `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, text inputs, risk badges, and transaction rows.
- **Action Pills & Floating Tags:** Fully circular / pill format (`9999px`) reserved specifically for quick filter pills, metric delta counters, and floating bottom navigation anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (High Velocity):**
  - Background: `#00F59B` solid fill.
  - Text: `#090D12` in `Outfit` semi-bold.
  - Border radius: `0.5rem` (or pill for quick UPI actions).
  - Hover / Active: Scale `0.98`, soft box-shadow `0 0 20px rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary (Ghost Glass):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Text: `#F8FAFC`.
  - Hover: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Metric Displays
- **Glass Portfolio Card:**
  - Base: `rgba(17, 23, 32, 0.75)` with `16px` backdrop filter blur.
  - Border: 1px continuous hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
  - Content: Prominent ₹ metric styled in `headline-lg` Outfit font, accompanied by a pill delta badge (`+14.8%` in `#00F59B`).
- **Interactive Sparkline Integration:** Charts within cards sit flush to the base or right-hand corner, rendered with a thin 2px gradient stroke fading into transparent zero-height fills.

### Semantic Chips & Badges
- **Risk Indicators:**
  - Low Risk / Safe: Background `rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.10)`, text `#00F59B`, 1px border `rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.25)`.
  - Watchlist / Medium: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.10)`, text `#F59E0B`, 1px border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
  - High Risk / Alert: Background `rgba(255, 75, 85, 0.10)`, text `#FF4B55`, 1px border `rgba(255, 75, 85, 0.25)`.
  - Shape: Rounded-pill with uppercase `label-md` tracking.

### Inputs & Forms
- **Monetary / Amount Field:**
  - Background: `#111720`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, transitioning to `#00F59B` upon focus with an exterior ring `rgba(0, 245, 155, 0.20)`.
  - Static Leading Adornment: ₹ symbol locked in `#94A3B8` (or active `#F8FAFC` once digits are populated).
  - Typography: `headline-md` for immediate readouts during transfers.

### Lists & Transaction Feeds
- **Transaction Line Item:**
  - Layout: Flex row, `space-md` padding along Y-axis, separated by hairline dividers `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
  - Left: Merchant/Receiver avatar enclosed in a 40x40px rounded container (`#182230`).
  - Center: Title (`title-md`) stacked over timestamp and UPI reference ID (`body-sm`).
  - Right: Net amount in `label-currency`. Reductions prefixed with `- ₹` in `#F8FAFC`; additions in `+ ₹` highlighted in `#00F59B`.

### Checkboxes & Switch Controls
- **Switch:** 
  - Track: Width `44px`, Height `24px`, background `#1E293B`, shifting to `#00F59B` when toggled.
  - Thumb: Width `20px`, Height `20px`, solid `#090D12` when active, `#94A3B8` when inactive.
